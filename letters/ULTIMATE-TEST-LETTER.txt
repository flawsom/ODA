In view of the above, the following directives are issued for strict compliance:

1. The Area General Managers of Raniganj-I, Dhanbad-IV, Bokaro-Steel City, and Jharia-III are directed to submit a detailed compliance report within 15 (fifteen) working days from the date of receipt of this letter, furnishing the following:

   (a) Completed Form PS-3 for all transferred employees (where pending);
   (b) Form A (Service Book Verification Certificate) attested by the Area Personnel Officer;
   (c) Form PS-4 (Last Pay Drawn Certificate) duly verified by the Accounts Department;
   (d) Transfer Certificate (TC-4) with acknowledgment receipt;
   (e) Medical fitness certificate from the designated CCL empanelled hospital;
   (f) Hostel accommodation clearance certificate (where applicable under Clause 7A of the Housing Policy);
   (g) No-dues certificate from the concerned Area Engineer (Mines/Opencast) certifying that all pit-passes, explosive licenses, and safety equipment have been returned.

2. In the case of Smt. Kamla Devi (S.No. 3 above), although the transfer documentation is complete, it has been noted that the line fitness certificate was issued by Dr. V.K. Mehra (MBBS, MD — Occupational Health), who is not a member of the panel constituted vide CCL/Medical/Panel/2025-26 dated 01.04.2025. Accordingly:

   (i)  The line fitness certificate is hereby declared as irregular under Clause 12(3)(b) of the CCL Medical Policy;
   (ii) Smt. Kamla Devi is directed to undergo a fresh medical examination at CCL Central Hospital, Rajendra Nagar, Ranchi, within 30 days;
   (iii) Until the fresh medical certificate is produced, her joining at Tirap Colliery shall be treated as provisional under Sub-Rule 4 of the CCL Transfer Regulations, 2019 (as amended in 2024).

3. The Chief of Personnel (CMPF), Regional Office-I, Ranchi, is hereby authorized to:

   (a) Initiate disciplinary proceedings under Rule 14(i) of the CCL Conduct, Discipline & Appeals Rules, 1978 (as amended) against the Area General Managers/Dy. General Managers responsible for the non-compliance at Raniganj-I and Bokaro-Steel City Areas;

   (b) Recover the overpayment of Transfer Allowance (TA) and Joining Allowance (JA) amounting to ₹1,84,700/- (Rupees One Lakh Eighty-Four Thousand Seven Hundred Only) from Shri Ramesh Chandra Singh (S.No. 1) and ₹92,350/- (Rupees Ninety-Two Thousand Three Hundred Fifty Only) from Shri Mohan Prasad Verma (S.No. 2), as per the computation sheet at Annexure-I;

   (c) Withhold the annual performance increment for the year 2026-27 of the erring Area Personnel Officers, pending finalization of the inquiry.

4. The Finance Department (CMPF/FIN/2026) is directed to:

   (a) Debit the accounts of the concerned Areas for the amounts mentioned in Para 3(b) above;
   (b) Ensure that no further TA/JA bills are processed for the transferred employees until all pending documents are received and verified;
   (c) Adjust the said amounts against the next quarterly fund allocation to the respective Areas under the CCL Working Capital Fund (WCF) as per Circular No. CIL/FIN/WCF/2026/089 dated 15.07.2026.

5. It is reiterated that all future inter-regional transfers shall strictly comply with the following mandatory requirements before the relieving order is issued:

   (i)   Completion of minimum 3 (three) years of service in the current Area (except in cases of mutual transfer under Clause 4C or compassionate grounds under Clause 4E);
   (ii)  Verification of service records by the Area Personnel Officer and certification by the Deputy General Manager (Personnel);
   (iii) Medical fitness certificate issued only by a panel-approved medical practitioner (the updated panel list is available on the CCL intranet portal at http://intranet.ccl.nic.in/HR/Medical/Panel-2026);
   (iv)  Clearance of all financial dues including but not limited to: Housing License Fee, Water & Electricity Charges, Club Membership Dues, Vehicle Parking Fees, and any outstanding advances under the CMPF Savings Scheme;
   (v)   Obtaining of NOC from the concerned Area General Manager, Area Chief of Personnel, and Area Chief of Finance;
   (vi)  Endorsement by the Member (Personnel), CCL Board, in cases where the transfer involves employees in the pay scale of ₹35,000-₹1,20,000 (IDA Pattern) or above.

6. The above directives shall take effect immediately and shall be applicable to all transfers processed on or after 01.04.2026. Transfers already completed before this date but where compliance is pending shall be regularized within 60 (sixty) days of receipt of this letter, failing which the concerned Area General Managers shall be held personally liable under the provisions of the CCL Delegation of Powers Order, 2023 (as amended vide Board Resolution dated 15.03.2026).

7. This issues with the approval of the Competent Authority vide Office Order No. CCL/OO/2026/Transfer/Comp/0047 dated 12.08.2026.
