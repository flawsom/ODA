Subject: Notice — Annual Examination Schedule for the Academic Session 2025-26

Sir,

In continuation of the examination calendar notified under Notification No. KU/EXAM/2026/88, all students of the first and second year are hereby informed that the annual examinations for the academic session 2025-26 shall commence from 01 October 2026 in the forenoon session.

The examination schedule, hall ticket distribution and the syllabus of the papers shall be communicated by the respective department heads in due course. Students are directed to deposit the examination fee of Rs. 1200/- with the examination section on or before 15 September 2026. Those who fail to deposit the fee within the prescribed period shall not be permitted to appear in the examinations.

Any discrepancy in the enrolment particulars of a student may be brought to the notice of the examination section immediately.
